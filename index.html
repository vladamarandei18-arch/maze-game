MAZE ARENA

❤️ Lives: 3
🎒 Weapon: None
👾 Enemies: 0
🚩 Level: 1
Find the Sword or Gun and reach the Green Exit!
Controls: Move with WASD / Arrows | Attack/Shoot with Space / F / Enter / Click | Drop with Q.
Rules: Carry 1 weapon at a time. Swapping drops your old weapon on your tile. Enemies search for weapons, avoid lava, and cannot enter blue Checkpoints. Sword enemies have 2 HP. Weapons burn if dropped in lava!
